# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

AI Coding Template is a technical collaboration space. Participants must treat
one another with respect, discuss evidence and tradeoffs in good faith, and make
room for people with different levels of experience and different technical
viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, actionable technical feedback.
- Critique ideas, code, and evidence without attacking the person.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Identify uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Follow repository security, approval, and disclosure rules.
- Accept moderation decisions and use the documented appeal route.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language or conduct based on identity or personal
  characteristics.
- Sexualized attention or content in project spaces.
- Publishing credentials, private reports, personal data, or confidential
  material.
- Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith.
- Deliberately bypassing repository safety, approval, or access controls.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, repository
contents, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Do not place sensitive personal details in a public issue. Ask `@Yzdyx` for a
private reporting channel using a minimal issue that contains no incident
details, or use a public contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub
profile.

For platform-level abuse, use
[GitHub's report abuse form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).
For a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer will limit access to reports, assess the available evidence, and
avoid conflicts of interest where practical. Possible actions include a private
correction, content removal, a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent
restriction from project spaces. The response and action will be proportionate
to the behavior, risk, and pattern of conduct.

Good-faith reports and appeals will not be used as grounds for retaliation.
